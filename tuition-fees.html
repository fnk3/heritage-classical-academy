Fast Facts

  • The registration fee is waived for all new students.
  • The student fee covers all books and supplies.
  • The tuition fee includes all field trips.
  • The tuition fee does not include school uniforms.
  • Tuition is due by July 1st. You may wish to enroll in our 10-month payment plan, with payments due on the first of each month, July through April.
