Our Mission

Heritage Classical Academy exists to glorify God by cultivating students of wisdom and virtue who embrace the joy of learning and are equipped to discern and defend truth in service to God and man.

Heritage Distinctives

An education shaped by three convictions

Classical

The classical method cultivates students of wisdom and virtue through a liberal arts education emphasizing truth, goodness, and beauty.

Welcome to Heritage

Education is about shaping who children are, not just what they know.

We believe children are entrusted to their parents as a gift from the Lord. At Heritage we use the time-tested classical model to create an environment where children become independent thinkers and effective communicators — with Christ at the center of all things.

We count it our joy to partner with parents in seeing children become young men and women equipped to live with purpose and passion for the good of man and the glory of God.
