Application Process

Our admissions process helps you determine if Heritage is a good fit for your family and your child.

Attend an Open House

The Heritage application process begins with attending one of our scheduled Open Houses — one of the most valuable steps a family can take. If you are unable to attend, please contact our Board President at sandra.george@heritageruston.com to schedule a one-on-one meeting. Sign up for an Open House here.

Open House dates: Tuesday, March 24, 2026  ·  Thursday, April 9, 2026 — both at 6:30 PM.

Submit an Application

Applications for the upcoming academic year are accepted beginning December 8, 2025. A $150 non-refundable fee is required to finalize each application. The first application deadline is January 29, 2026 for primary consideration; the second deadline is March 19, 2026. After that, we offer rolling admissions based on availability.

Begin an Online Application

Readiness Assessment

Once a complete application is submitted, a readiness assessment will be scheduled for your child. All testing must be completed before moving to the family interview.

Family Interview

Once your application and student assessment are reviewed, you may be invited to participate in a family interview. This vital step provides an opportunity for our administration to learn more about your family, your student, and your interest in classical Christian education.
