Tuition Assistance

We are committed to making a Heritage education accessible to families who desire it.

HCA is thrilled to be able to offer two grammar school scholarships beginning the new school year, '26–'27.

Families are asked to apply for either the ACE and/or the Gator Fund scholarships first. Once a family has submitted their application for one or both of those scholarships and received a decision notice, they can then apply for the HCA Grammar School Scholarships should they be denied one of those state-funded scholarships.

The application process for ACE and Gator begins the first week in February. We encourage you to visit the links below to help you gather required documents and evaluate your family's eligibility. These scholarships are awarded on a first-come, first-served basis, so we encourage you to apply as soon as possible.

Once you receive a decision letter, email a copy to sandra.george@heritageruston.com and a link will be sent to you to apply for the HCA scholarships.

HCA Awarded Amount: $2,000 per year for any year a child is enrolled in Kindergarten–5th grade.
