Welcome to Heritage

We are honored that you are considering Heritage Classical Academy.

We believe children are entrusted to their parents as a gift from the Lord. Making the decision concerning where your child will attend school is an important decision for every family. We are honored that you are considering Heritage.

At Heritage we use the time-tested classical model of education that creates an environment where children can become independent thinkers and effective communicators. What makes Heritage different is our belief that education is about shaping who children are, not just what they know. Our commitment to pointing students to see Christ as the center of all things and our belief that He is the source of all truth creates an environment at Heritage that allows us to cultivate wisdom and virtue in the minds and hearts of the children entrusted to us by their parents.

Heritage holds that trust with a deep sense of humility and responsibility. We see it as our purpose and count it as our joy to partner with parents in seeing your children become young men and women who are well-equipped to live with purpose and passion for the good of man and the glory of God.

Again, we are honored that you are considering Heritage, and we invite you to contact us for more information or to request a visit.

For His Glory,
Sandra George
Heritage Board President
