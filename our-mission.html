Our Mission

Heritage Classical Academy exists to glorify God by cultivating students of wisdom and virtue who embrace the joy of learning and are equipped to discern and defend truth in service to God and man.

Core Values

The founders of Heritage are committed to providing an education that includes:

  • Honoring God through humility, sacrifice, and service.
  • Teaching from a Christ-centered worldview with all subjects integrated around the Word of God.
  • Recognizing the primacy of parents in their child's education.
  • Employing outstanding teachers and administrators who confess and demonstrate the saving work of Jesus Christ in their lives, a reverence for the classical tradition, and a lifetime love of learning.
  • Encouraging each student to grow in his or her personal relationship with Jesus Christ.
  • Providing an education that embraces the liberal arts tradition and is committed to developing students through the instruction of grammar, logic, and rhetoric.
  • Emphasizing the principles of Charlotte Mason as they pertain to discipline, curriculum, and methodology.

Statement of Faith

  • We believe in God almighty, maker of heaven and earth. Therefore, we approach all truth as edifying for those who believe in God, and we strive to understand every aspect of the world as part of God's creation.
  • We believe creation — including human thought and culture — is good yet fallen from its proper relation to God. Therefore, we approach the best of human culture as an expression of creation yearning to be reunited to God, and we recognize that our own understanding of God and creation is limited.
  • We believe in the Lord Jesus Christ, God's only Son, one in being with the Father, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of a virgin.
  • We believe God acted decisively in history through Jesus, who suffered, died, was buried, and on the third day rose again, ascended to heaven, and who with the Father will come to judge mankind.
  • We believe that through Jesus Christ, God reconciled the world to himself by granting the remission of sin, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Therefore, the story of God's saving work in history and promised consummation is essential to our understanding of the world.
  • We believe God continues to work in history through his Holy Spirit to restore the image of God in us. Therefore, we trust in God's indwelling presence to empower us to discern truth, excellence, and beauty, and to do justice, love mercy, and walk humbly before God.
  • We believe Holy Scripture is inspired by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness. In response to the goodness and grace of the Triune God made manifest in creation, Christ, and the Holy Spirit, we develop our minds and our hearts that we may be good stewards of God's creation and faithful bearers of Christ's image in love and service to one another.

Growth Plans

The long-term goal of Heritage is to become a K–12 school with a Pre-K 4 program. Each year the Heritage Board will prayerfully seek God's guidance in beginning the next grade levels until we reach this goal.

The Board will also begin, by the 2027–28 school year, to develop a need-based scholarship fund to aid families with tuition.

We covet your prayers as we continue to move forward following God's leadership and seeking to bring Him glory.
