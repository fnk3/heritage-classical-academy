Tuition Assistance

We are committed to making a Heritage education accessible to families who desire it.

HCA is thrilled to be able to offer two grammar school scholarships beginning the new school year, '26–'27.

Families are asked to apply for the ACE, Arete, and/or LA Gator scholarships first. Once a family has submitted their application for one or more of those scholarships and received a decision notice, they can then apply for the HCA Grammar School Scholarships should they be denied one of those state-funded scholarships.

The application process for ACE, Arete, and LA Gator begins the first week in February. We encourage you to visit the links below to help you gather required documents and evaluate your family's eligibility. These scholarships are awarded on a first-come, first-served basis, so we encourage you to apply as soon as possible.

Once you receive a decision letter, contact our Board President at sandra.george@heritageruston.com to discuss HCA scholarship options.

HCA Awarded Amount: $2,000 per year for any year a child is enrolled in Kindergarten–5th grade.
