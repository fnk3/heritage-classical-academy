Give Through a Tax Credit

Fund scholarships at little or no net cost

Louisiana and federal law let you direct tax dollars into K–12 scholarships. Gifts go to an approved Scholarship Granting Organization, which awards need-based scholarships that families may use at Heritage Classical Academy.

Available Now

Louisiana Tuition Donation Credit

Donate to a Louisiana School Tuition Organization such as ACE Scholarships or Arete Scholars and receive a 95% Louisiana state income tax credit. The remaining portion may qualify for a federal charitable deduction. Unused credit carries forward up to three years.

Give through ACE Give through Arete

Beginning January 1, 2027

Federal Education Freedom Tax Credit

Starting in 2027, individual donors receive a dollar-for-dollar federal tax credit of up to $1,700 per year for gifts to an approved Scholarship Granting Organization. Unused credit carries forward up to five years. Louisiana has opted in to the program.
