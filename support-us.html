Help Support Us

Three ways to partner with Heritage as we grow.

Pray

Our faculty and staff covet your prayers. We desire to exemplify Christ-likeness in our personal and professional lives, relying on His help to model the wisdom and virtue we want to see in our students. "The student is not above the teacher, but everyone who is fully trained will be like their teacher." (Luke 6:40) Please join us in prayer for the right individuals to fill key faculty and staff roles, and for our fundraising goals to be met.

Volunteer

We value every member of the Heritage community, and we look forward to having parents support our school, faculty, and staff by volunteering in a variety of ways throughout the school year.

Give
