Frequently Asked Questions

Where are you located?
Heritage is located at 5200 Hwy. 167 North, Ruston, LA, inside Calvary Baptist Church.
How do I apply?
See our application process for the four steps to enrollment.
What are your school hours?
Kindergarten: 8:00 – 12:45.
First & Second Grade: 8:00 – 2:45 Monday–Thursday, 8:00 – 12:45 on Friday. Optional Extended Day is offered until 2:45 M–F.
Do you offer an extended day program?
Yes. We offer an optional 12:45 – 2:45 Extended Day Program for Kindergarten students, with a combination of outdoor and indoor free play, story time, games, and rest. Options are M–F or T/Th, with an additional fee. First and Second Grade students may stay for Extended Day on Fridays.
Does Heritage have a religious affiliation?
A biblical worldview is woven throughout everything we do at Heritage. Calvary Baptist Church brought together individuals representing a variety of Protestant churches in our community to form HCA. We are united around the truth of the gospel and a desire to shape the affections of our students toward Jesus Christ.
What are your class sizes?
Class sizes are limited to 14 students.
How much does it cost?
See our Tuition & Fees page for full details.
Do you offer financial assistance?
Yes — we are proud to partner with the ACE Scholarship and LA Gator Fund initiatives, and offer HCA Grammar School Scholarships. See Tuition Assistance for details.
